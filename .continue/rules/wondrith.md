For Wondrith tasks:
- This is a Pixi.js spatial task app
- Ideas are clouds above the conversion band
- Tasks are towers below the conversion band
- Dragging a cloud through the conversion band converts it into a task
- Motivation tags can be inferred from conversion columns
- Use appState as the single source of truth
- Keep business logic out of renderers
- Put dragging, selection, and conversion logic in controllers/interactions
- Prefer the current milestone over speculative future systems
